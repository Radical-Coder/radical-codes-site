The sprint stays narrow on purpose.

The goal is not to automate a business in one pass. It is to ship one lane small enough to verify and useful enough to keep.

01

Map the source

Name the inputs, owner, examples, sensitive data, destination, and acceptance checks.

02

Build the lane

Structure the data, run the AI step, surface exceptions, and preserve source context.

03

Keep review visible

Let a human approve, edit, reject, or escalate before output reaches a real system.

04

Leave proof behind

Ship logs, examples, docs, handoff notes, and the next improvement list.

Good fit

  • The workflow happens every week.
  • The app exists but trust is not there yet.
  • A person already reviews or approves the work.
  • The output needs to land in a real system.
  • Mistakes matter enough that blind automation would be irresponsible.

Not a fit

  • No repeated workflow or accountable owner.
  • A generic chatbot with no operational destination.
  • AI making final legal, medical, financial, or hiring decisions.
  • Unsupervised external messages without review.
  • A full rebuild when a triage pass is the honest first step.
